Turning Imagination
Into Intelligent Machines

Imagination Inventors is a student-led robotics and AI initiative founded by Aman Vishwas. We build real, working intelligent systems — not just ideas. Our focus is on combining hardware, embedded systems, computer vision, and voice intelligence into machines that interact naturally with humans. From physical AI robots like AmVish to experimental interactive systems, every project is built hands-on, tested in real environments, and designed to solve real problems. We believe innovation doesn’t require a big lab — it requires vision, consistency, and the courage to build.
